== Sekce 5 ==
CZ: Hledání levharta není nic snadného. V území obývaném lvy je pro menší šelmy životní nutností pohybovat se skrytě, jinak by se mohli sami stát kořistí. Zejména mláďata levharta nebo geparda se často stávají obětí lvů, kteří se tak zbavují potravní konkurence.
EN: Finding a leopard is no easy task. In territory inhabited by lions, moving covertly is a matter of survival for smaller carnivores, as they could otherwise become prey themselves. Leopard or cheetah cubs in particular often fall victim to lions, who use this to eliminate competition for food.

EN: The story of this pair does not have a happy ending. According to our guides, we were the last ones to see the mother and her cub. We can only hope that this is not the case and that they moved to an entirely different area.
